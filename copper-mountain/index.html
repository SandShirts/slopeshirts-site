Copper Mountain Shirts & Hoodies

Copper Mountain sits right off I-70 in Summit County, at the foot of Vail Pass and just a few miles from Frisco. The mountain is famous for terrain that sorts itself out naturally: gentler runs on the west side, cruisers through the middle and steeper, more advanced terrain to the east, so a mixed group can split up and still meet at the bottom.

Up top, the bowls off Union Peak and Copper Peak open wide above treeline, and snowcat rides head out to Tucker Mountain. Down below, West Village, Center Village and East Village give the resort its walkable base, and Woodward Copper has long made it a favorite with park skiers and riders. A soft Copper Mountain hoodie is an easy way to bring a little of Summit County home. Tees start at $29.99, and shipping is free on orders over $100.

Yes. Use the Make It Your Own feature on the design page after choosing a town and type in the name you want, like Copper, Summit County or Frisco.

Will the print feel stiff or heavy?
No. Our designs are thin line art printed in-house, and the shirt color shows through the design, so the print stays soft and feels like part of the fabric.

Yes. Every order is printed in-house to order, so we can control the quality, print placement and finish.
