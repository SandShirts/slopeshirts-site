Truckee Shirts & Hoodies

Truckee is the old railroad and logging town just north of Lake Tahoe, set in the Sierra Nevada along Interstate 80 just below Donner Pass. Its historic downtown, Commercial Row, faces the train tracks, where Amtrak's California Zephyr still stops at the Truckee depot, and the Truckee River runs right past town on its way from Lake Tahoe toward Reno. Just west of downtown sits Donner Lake, and Donner Memorial State Park on its shore tells the story of the Donner Party and the emigrants who crossed these mountains in the 1840s.

For skiers, Truckee is surrounded by mountains. Northstar California is a few miles south of town, Palisades Tahoe in Olympic Valley, host of the 1960 Winter Olympics, is a short drive south on Highway 89, and Sugar Bowl, Boreal and the Royal Gorge cross-country trails sit up on Donner Summit. The Tahoe Donner neighborhood even has its own small downhill area and cross-country center. A soft Truckee hoodie is an easy way to remember a Tahoe ski week.
