Deer Valley Shirts & Hoodies

Deer Valley sits on the east side of Park City, Utah, in the Wasatch Mountains less than an hour from Salt Lake City. The resort opened in 1981 and built its name on carefully groomed runs, a limit on daily skier numbers and lodges known for their food, including the turkey chili that has become a Deer Valley tradition. It is also one of the few ski resorts in the country that is still skiers only, with no snowboarding on the mountain.

The terrain spreads across several peaks, including Bald Eagle Mountain above Snow Park Lodge, Bald Mountain, Flagstaff Mountain and the steeper chutes of Empire Canyon, with Silver Lake Village tucked in mid-mountain. During the 2002 Salt Lake City Winter Olympics, Deer Valley hosted the moguls, aerials and slalom events, and its Champion run still brings freestyle World Cup competition back most winters. Historic Main Street in Park City is just down the road. A soft Deer Valley hoodie is an easy way to remember a Utah ski trip. Tees start at $29.99, and shipping is free on orders over $100.
