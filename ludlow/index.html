Ludlow & Okemo Shirts & Hoodies

Ludlow is a small Vermont mill town on the Black River, where Route 100 and Route 103 meet in the hills of Windsor County. Okemo Mountain rises right behind Main Street, so the ski resort and the town feel like one place: you can grab breakfast downtown and be on the lifts a few minutes later. Okemo is known for wide, well-groomed trails and heated bubble chairlifts, and the Jackson Gore base area on the north side of the mountain adds a second village of lodges and lifts.

Away from the slopes, the old mill buildings and church steeples along Main Street give Ludlow its classic New England look. Just north on Route 100, a chain of lakes including Lake Rescue and Echo Lake freezes over for winter, and President Calvin Coolidge's birthplace at Plymouth Notch is a short drive up the road. Killington is under an hour north, which makes Ludlow an easy home base for a Vermont ski week. A soft Ludlow or Okemo hoodie is the right souvenir for the drive home.
