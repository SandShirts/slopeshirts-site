Durango Shirts & Hoodies

Durango sits along the Animas River in Southwest Colorado, where the San Juan Mountains start to rise. It is a real working mountain town with a historic downtown on Main Avenue, Fort Lewis College up on the mesa, and the Durango & Silverton Narrow Gauge Railroad, whose steam trains have been running up the Animas canyon toward Silverton since the 1880s.

In winter, most skiers head about 25 miles north on Highway 550 to Purgatory Resort, known for its stepped terrain, sunny San Juan views and a laid-back feel that keeps families coming back. Locals also have Chapman Hill, a small city-run ski hill and ice rink right in town. A soft Durango hoodie is an easy way to bring a little of Southwest Colorado home.
