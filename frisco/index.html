Frisco Shirts & Hoodies

Frisco is the small mountain town at the center of Summit County, tucked between the Tenmile Range and the shore of Dillon Reservoir. Its historic Main Street runs from the foot of Mount Royal and Peak One down toward the lake, lined with restaurants, shops and old mining-era cabins, and the Frisco Historic Park & Museum keeps a cluster of those early buildings on display right in town.

For skiers, Frisco is the home base that puts you within a short drive of Frisco, Breckenridge, Keystone and Arapahoe Basin, with the free Summit Stage bus and the paved recpath connecting the towns around the county. Closer to home, the Frisco Adventure Park on the Peninsula has a tubing hill, a small beginner ski hill and groomed Nordic and snowshoe trails along the reservoir. A soft Frisco hoodie is an easy way to remember a Summit County ski week. Tees start at $29.99, and shipping is free on orders over $100.
