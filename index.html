Our Story

Mountain-Born,
Lodge-Made

Some of my favorite memories happened in the mountains: cold mornings on the chairlift, fresh snow, and warming up by the fire after a long day. But most ski-town shirts I found looked great on the rack and then felt stiff, heavy, and scratchy the second I put them on.

Slope Shirts is the winter sister brand of Sand Shirts. It's the same small business and the same soft, comfortable apparel, now made for the ski towns you love. These are shirts and hoodies you'll want to wear on the mountain, in the lodge, on the drive home, and every day after.

Every product is carefully selected for comfort, quality, and feel before it ever gets printed. We use a high-quality DTF printing process and make everything in-house so we can control the quality every step of the way. Our designs are printed so the shirt color shows through, which keeps them soft and part of the fabric. This isn't fast fashion or generic print-on-demand apparel.

No cheap gift-shop materials. No stiff, rough, scratchy fabrics. Just soft custom ski town apparel inspired by the mountains you love most.

Stephanie
